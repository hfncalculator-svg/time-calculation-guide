# Time Calculation Guide: Formulas and Examples

This guide explains simple ways to calculate future times, past times, elapsed time, and work hours.

## 1. Calculate a Future Time

To find a future time, add the required number of hours and minutes to the starting time.

**Example:**

Starting time: 10:00 AM  
Hours to add: 5  
Result: 3:00 PM

You can also use the [Hours From Now Calculator](https://hoursfromnowcalculator.com/) to calculate a future time and date online.

## 2. Calculate a Past Time

To find a past time, subtract the required hours or minutes from the current time.

**Example:**

Starting time: 6:00 PM  
Hours to subtract: 3  
Result: 3:00 PM

Try the [Hours Ago Calculator](https://hoursfromnowcalculator.com/hours-ago-calculator/) to find a past time.

## 3. Calculate Elapsed Time

Elapsed time is the difference between a start time and an end time.

**Example:**

Start: 9:30 AM  
End: 1:00 PM  
Elapsed time: 3 hours and 30 minutes

If the end time falls on the next day, account for the date change when calculating the total.

## 4. Convert Minutes to Decimal Hours

Divide the number of minutes by 60.

**Example:**

90 minutes ÷ 60 = 1.5 hours

Decimal hours are useful for timesheets, payroll calculations, and project tracking.

## 5. Calculate Work Hours

Subtract the starting time from the finishing time, then subtract unpaid breaks.

**Example:**

Start: 9:00 AM  
Finish: 5:00 PM  
Unpaid break: 1 hour  
Total work time: 7 hours

## Conclusion

Clear time calculations help with everyday planning, work schedules, meetings, and timesheets. Always account for dates when a calculation crosses midnight.
